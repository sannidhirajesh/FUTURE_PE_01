# Prompt Engineering Documentation

## Project: Bean & Bloom Café Website Copy Generator

### Prompt 1: Homepage Content

**Objective:** Generate engaging homepage content.

**Prompt:**

Act as a professional website copywriter.

Create homepage content for Bean & Bloom Café, a cozy,
modern coffee shop.

Target audience:
Students, working professionals, and coffee lovers.

Brand tone:
Warm, friendly, welcoming, and modern.

Include:
1. A catchy headline.
2. A compelling subheadline.
3. A short introduction.
4. A unique value proposition.
5. Three call-to-action buttons.

Keep the content natural, engaging, and suitable for
a real business website.

---

### Prompt 2: Menu Descriptions

**Objective:** Create attractive menu descriptions.

**Prompt:**

Act as an expert food and beverage copywriter.

Write appealing menu descriptions for Bean & Bloom Café.

Include:
- Espresso
- Cappuccino
- Latte
- Cold Coffee
- Chocolate Croissant

For each item:
1. Write a short, attractive description.
2. Highlight the flavor and experience.
3. Use simple, customer-friendly language.

Avoid exaggerated claims and keep descriptions concise.

---

### Prompt 3: About Us Section

**Objective:** Create a warm brand story.

**Prompt:**

Act as a brand storyteller.

Write an About Us section for Bean & Bloom Café.

The café is a cozy, welcoming space for students,
professionals, and coffee lovers.

Use a warm, friendly tone.

Explain the café's atmosphere and the experience
customers can expect.

Keep the content engaging and easy to read.

---

### Prompt 4: Services Section

**Objective:** Describe the café's services.

**Prompt:**

Act as a professional website copywriter.

Write a Services section for Bean & Bloom Café.

Include:
1. Dine-in experience.
2. Takeaway coffee.
3. Coffee and snacks.

Use clear headings and short descriptions.

Maintain a warm and welcoming brand voice.

---

### Prompt 5: Call-to-Action Content

**Objective:** Encourage customers to explore the café.

**Prompt:**

Act as a conversion-focused website copywriter.

Create compelling calls to action for Bean & Bloom Café.

Include CTAs for:
1. Exploring the menu.
2. Visiting the café.
3. Contacting the business.

Keep the language friendly, clear, and action-oriented.

Provide three variations for each CTA.
